Controls

  • Drag a face to turn that layer (flick for a quick turn)
  • Drag the background, or right-drag / two fingers, to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom
  • U D L R F B turn a face clockwise; hold Shift for counter-clockwise
  • M E S turn the middle slices
  • Backspace or Ctrl+Z undoes your last move
  • Scramble starts a timed solve; Solve plays your moves back to the start

WebGL isn’t available in this browser, so the cube can’t be drawn.
